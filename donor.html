How Donor Coordination Works

VitalLoop acts as a discovery and coordination layer.

👤

1. Create Profile

Add your blood group, city and availability to create a donor profile.

🔎

2. Discover Requests

Relevant blood requests can be matched using blood group and location information.

🏥

3. Authorized Donation

If coordination proceeds, donation takes place at an authorized healthcare facility or blood centre.

🛡️ Blood Safety First
VitalLoop does not collect, test, store, transport or release blood. Donor eligibility, medical screening, infection testing, compatibility and actual blood donation are handled by authorized healthcare professionals and blood centres.
