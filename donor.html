How Smart Donor Matching Works

RaktSathi can use request requirements to identify potential donor resources. Matching does not confirm eligibility or guarantee donation.

01 Blood Group
Match the requested blood group with registered donor information.
02 Location
Prioritise potentially relevant geographic resources.
03 Availability
Consider the donor's registered availability status.

Donor Coordination Workflow

A responsible matching process keeps verification before any medical decision.

STEP 01

Emergency Request

A blood requirement is created.

STEP 02

Smart Matching

Potential donor resources are identified.

STEP 03

Contact & Verify

Appropriate verification is handled by responsible people and facilities.

STEP 04

Facility Coordination

Actual donation and medical procedures remain with authorised facilities.

Important Safety Notice: RaktSathi is a coordination prototype and does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. Donor registration does not mean a person is medically eligible to donate. Final eligibility, screening, compatibility and donation decisions must be made by authorised healthcare professionals and blood centres. Availability information should be independently verified.
