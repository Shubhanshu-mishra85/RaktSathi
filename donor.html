🔄 How Donor Matching Works

1
Donor Registers

Donor provides blood group and location.

2
Blood Request Arrives

A family raises a blood requirement.

3
Potential Match

System can identify potential donors based on available information.

4
Contact & Coordination

The potential donor can be contacted through an appropriate authorized workflow.

5
Medical Screening

The blood centre decides donor eligibility and performs the required procedures.

🩸 Important: Matching ≠ Guaranteed Blood

A potential donor match does not mean that blood is immediately available or medically compatible. Donation, testing, compatibility and blood issue must be handled by an authorized blood centre.

🛡️ Safety First

VitalLoop does not decide whether someone can donate. A qualified healthcare professional performs donor screening before donation.
