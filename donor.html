Safety & Responsibility

RaktSathi does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. It is a coordination and information prototype. All medical decisions, donor eligibility checks, compatibility checks and blood handling must be performed by authorized healthcare facilities and qualified professionals.
