Important Safety Notice:
RaktSathi is a technology prototype for discovery and coordination. Creating a request does not guarantee donor availability, blood availability or fulfilment. RaktSathi does not collect, test, store, transport, release or distribute blood. Blood collection, testing, compatibility, storage and transfusion are handled by authorised blood centres and qualified healthcare professionals. In a genuine medical emergency, contact the treating healthcare facility and appropriate emergency services immediately.
