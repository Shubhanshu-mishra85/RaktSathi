Important Safety Notice:
RaktSathi is a discovery and coordination prototype. A request does not guarantee blood availability, donor availability or fulfilment. Actual blood collection, testing, compatibility, storage, release and transfusion are handled by authorised blood centres and qualified healthcare professionals. For a genuine medical emergency, contact the treating healthcare facility and appropriate emergency services immediately.
