ABOUT VITALLOOP

Making Blood Resource Discovery Simpler.

VitalLoop is a free healthcare technology prototype designed to help families discover relevant blood resources, raise blood requests and coordinate with potential donors and authorized healthcare resources through one connected experience.

THE PROBLEM

When an emergency happens, finding the right resource can be difficult.

Families may have to contact multiple people, hospitals or blood centres while dealing with an emergency. VitalLoop aims to make this discovery and coordination process simpler.

🚑

Emergency Situation

For example, after an accident, a patient's family may urgently need a particular blood group and may not know where relevant resources or potential donors can be found.

🔎

Resource Discovery

VitalLoop can organize blood-resource information so that users can search using blood group and location.

🤝

Potential Donor Coordination

If a suitable family donor is not available, the system can help identify potential donors based on the information available in the prototype.

🏥

Authorized Healthcare Role

Blood collection, testing, compatibility decisions, storage and issue remain the responsibility of authorized blood centres and healthcare professionals.

HOW IT WORKS

From request to coordination

📝

1. Request

Family raises a blood requirement.

🩸

2. Blood Group

Required blood group and units are entered.

📍

3. Location

Relevant location is provided.

🔎

4. Discovery

Potential donors and resources can be identified.

🤝

5. Coordination

The family can follow the appropriate healthcare workflow.

PROCESSING CHARGES

Blood is not treated as a product for sale.

Where applicable, authorized blood centres may charge regulated processing/service-related charges. The exact amount depends on the blood centre, component and applicable rules.

Government / Public Centre

As applicable

Charges and concessions can vary by centre and patient eligibility. Users should confirm the current amount with the concerned authorized blood centre.

Financial Assistance

Proposed

VitalLoop's SIH concept proposes a financial-assistance mechanism for eligible low-income/BPL beneficiaries, subject to verification, funding and applicable rules.

Private / Other Centre

Centre-specific

Applicable charges can differ by centre and blood component. Always verify the current official rate before making any payment.

💚 VitalLoop Assistance Concept

For the SIH prototype, VitalLoop proposes up to 50% assistance for verified eligible BPL/low-income users, subject to a future approved funding and verification model. This is a project proposal, not a currently guaranteed government benefit.

⚠️ Important: Do not make a payment to a donor. Any applicable charge should be paid only through the authorized blood centre or healthcare facility according to its official process.

🛡️ Safety & Trust

VitalLoop is designed as a discovery and coordination layer. It does not replace doctors, hospitals or authorized blood centres.

  • No guarantee of blood availability.
  • No independent blood collection or storage by VitalLoop.
  • No independent compatibility decision.
  • Final screening and transfusion decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Prototype/demo data should never be treated as live medical availability.
  • Real patient information should only be handled through a properly secured production backend.
