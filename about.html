WHAT IS RAKTSATHI?

A Coordination Layer for Blood Emergencies

RaktSathi focuses on the journey around a blood requirement — from creating a structured request to discovering potential resources and verifying information.

🩸

The Problem

During an emergency, people may need to search across different sources, contact multiple facilities and verify information under significant time pressure.

🔗

Our Approach

RaktSathi brings discovery, potential donor coordination, emergency requests, tracking and verification guidance into one structured digital experience.

OUR SOLUTION

From Emergency Need to Coordinated Action

The platform is designed around a clear, step-by-step emergency coordination journey.

🚨

Emergency Request

Capture important requirement details in a structured digital request.

🔎

✓

INNOVATION LAYER

More Than a Blood Search Page

RaktSathi is designed as a coordination layer around the emergency blood journey.

Emergency-First Design

The experience begins with the emergency requirement instead of making users navigate multiple disconnected sources.

Potential Matching

Blood group, component, location and availability signals can be used to organize potential resources.

Verification Layer

The system clearly distinguishes potential information from professionally verified information.

Status Tracking

A structured request journey provides better visibility from creation to closure.

Decision-Support Ready

Future versions can add analytics and AI/ML decision-support capabilities.

Modular Architecture

Emergency requests, discovery, donors, camps, education and assistance can evolve as connected modules.

🛡️

Responsible Healthcare Technology

RaktSathi is a coordination and information prototype. It does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. It does not diagnose patients, prescribe medicines, determine medical compatibility or guarantee blood availability.

✓

Verification Matters

Demonstration resource and camp information must be verified with the relevant authorized healthcare facility or organizer before relying on it.
