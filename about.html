ABOUT RAKTSATHI

Intelligent Blood Emergency Coordination

RaktSathi is a patient-centric coordination layer designed to connect emergency blood requirements with potential resources, donor networks, healthcare facilities and verification workflows.

What is RaktSathi?

RaktSathi is a healthcare technology prototype focused on improving the journey from a blood requirement to coordinated resource discovery. Instead of acting as a blood bank, RaktSathi works as a digital coordination layer that helps structure requests, discover potential resources, identify matching donor information and guide users toward appropriate verification. The core philosophy is simple: Discover → Match → Coordinate → Verify → Resolve.

The Problem

Blood emergencies involve multiple people, facilities and information sources.

PROBLEM 01

Fragmented Information

Patients and attendants may need to search across multiple sources while trying to understand where relevant blood resources may exist.

PROBLEM 02

Emergency Time Pressure

During urgent situations, manually coordinating requirements, locations and potential resources can become difficult.

PROBLEM 03

Verification Gap

Online availability information may not represent real-time confirmed availability, making verification an essential part of the workflow.

The RaktSathi Solution

One coordinated workflow instead of disconnected steps.

SOLUTION 01

Emergency Request

Create a structured request containing blood group, component, location, urgency and healthcare facility.

SOLUTION 02

Smart Resource Discovery

Search potential blood-centre resources using structured requirements and location information.

SOLUTION 03

Potential Donor Matching

Identify potentially relevant registered donor resources using blood group, location and availability information.

SOLUTION 04

Verification Workflow

Keep facility verification between discovery and any real-world medical action.

SOLUTION 05

Request Tracking

SOLUTION 06

Smart Assistant

Guide users toward the appropriate RaktSathi module based on their requirement.

Our Vision

Connecting People. Saving Lives.

Build a responsible digital coordination network where emergency blood requirements can be structured, potential resources can be discovered and the right healthcare stakeholders can be connected through a transparent verification workflow.

Safety & Responsibility: RaktSathi is a healthcare coordination prototype. It does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. It does not guarantee blood availability and does not make medical compatibility, donor eligibility or treatment decisions. Actual blood collection, testing, storage, compatibility assessment and transfusion are handled by authorised healthcare professionals and facilities.
