OUR VISION

What is RaktSathi?

RaktSathi is designed around one simple idea: during a blood emergency, people should not have to navigate disconnected information sources manually.

🚨

Emergency First

🔎

✓

Verification Focus

THE PROBLEM

What Happens During a Blood Emergency?

Finding a blood resource can become difficult when information is fragmented and time is critical.

01

Fragmented Information

Patients and families may need to search across different sources to identify potential blood resources.

02

Time Pressure

During emergencies, manually contacting multiple resources can increase coordination difficulty.

03

Verification Gap

Online information may not represent current availability, making direct verification essential.

Responsible Healthcare Technology

RaktSathi is a coordination and information prototype. It does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. It does not diagnose patients, prescribe treatment or guarantee blood availability. Final medical, compatibility and blood-handling decisions remain with authorized healthcare facilities and qualified professionals.

Relationship With Existing Blood Ecosystems

RaktSathi is designed as a complementary coordination layer rather than a replacement for authorized blood-bank and healthcare systems. Its value is in connecting an emergency request with discovery, potential matching, verification and tracking.
