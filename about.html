OUR PURPOSE

What is RaktSathi?

RaktSathi focuses on the coordination gap that can occur during urgent blood requirements.

🚨

The Problem

During emergencies, families may need to search across multiple sources for blood-centre information, potential donors and healthcare support.

🧠

The Approach

RaktSathi brings discovery, matching, coordination and request tracking into one connected workflow.

🤝

The Goal

Make the journey from an emergency blood requirement to verified healthcare coordination more organised and easier to navigate.

CORE WORKFLOW

RESPONSIBLE TECHNOLOGY

Technology With Human Oversight

RaktSathi supports discovery and coordination. It does not replace medical professionals or authorised blood-centre processes.

🔐

Privacy Awareness

Sensitive information should be collected only when required and handled responsibly.

🏥

Professional Verification

Final blood availability, compatibility and clinical decisions remain with authorised professionals.

⚠️

No False Guarantees

Potential matches and availability information must always be independently verified.

👨‍⚕️

Human-Centred Design

Technology assists coordination while healthcare professionals remain responsible for medical decisions.

OUR VISION

Make Blood Emergency Coordination Simpler

RaktSathi aims to create a connected digital experience where emergency blood requirements, potential resources, donor networks and healthcare facilities can coordinate more efficiently.
