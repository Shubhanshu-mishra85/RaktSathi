Blood Safety Education

Know Before You Donate

Education can help people make safer and more informed healthcare decisions.

01

Who Can Donate?

Eligibility depends on factors such as age, health, medical history and the rules of the authorized blood centre.

02

Blood Testing

Collected blood is screened by authorized facilities before it can be used for transfusion.

03

Compatibility Matters

Blood components must be appropriately matched and released according to medical protocols.

AI Assistant

AI is intended for educational first-level guidance, not diagnosis or replacement of medical professionals.

Hello! I'm the VitalLoop AI assistant. I can provide general healthcare information. For emergencies or serious symptoms, contact a healthcare professional immediately.

Safety Comes First.

VitalLoop is designed as a discovery and coordination layer. It does not itself store, test, transport, release or distribute blood.

✓ Authorized blood centres remain responsible for collection and testing.
✓ Blood availability must be verified with the concerned centre.
✓ Compatibility decisions remain under medical protocols.
✓ AI does not replace doctors or emergency services.

Important

VitalLoop is a technology prototype concept. Actual blood donation, testing, storage, release and transfusion must be carried out by authorized healthcare facilities and trained professionals according to applicable medical rules.
