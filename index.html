Built With Responsibility

RaktSathi is designed as a coordination and discovery layer—not as a replacement for authorized healthcare systems.

Verification First

Blood availability, eligibility, compatibility and final allocation must be confirmed by the relevant authorized healthcare facility or blood centre.

No Blood Operations

RaktSathi does not test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. These processes remain with authorized facilities and qualified professionals.

No Availability Guarantee

Information shown by the prototype should never be interpreted as a guarantee of real-time blood availability.

Human Oversight

Critical healthcare decisions remain under qualified professional and institutional oversight.
