Important Safety Notice:
RaktSathi is a technology prototype for blood-resource discovery and coordination. It does not collect, test, store, transport, release or distribute blood. Actual blood availability, donor eligibility, compatibility, collection, testing, storage and transfusion are handled by authorised blood centres and qualified healthcare professionals.
