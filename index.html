Responsible Healthcare Technology

RaktSathi is a coordination and information prototype. It does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. It does not diagnose patients, prescribe medicines, determine medical compatibility or guarantee blood availability. Actual blood operations and medical decisions remain with authorized healthcare facilities and qualified professionals.

Verification Matters

Resource and camp information shown in the prototype may be demonstration data. Always verify current availability and relevant details directly with the authorized healthcare facility or organizer.
