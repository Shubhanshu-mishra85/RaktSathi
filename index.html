Why RaktSathi?

Designed to go beyond a basic blood-availability search interface.

INNOVATION 01

Emergency-First

The journey begins with the patient's requirement instead of disconnected searches.

INNOVATION 02

Smart Matching

Blood group, component, location and registered availability can act as matching signals.

INNOVATION 03

Verification Layer

Potential resources are clearly separated from confirmed medical or facility decisions.

INNOVATION 04

Request Tracking

A structured status journey makes the coordination process easier to understand.

INNOVATION 05

Decision-Support Ready

Future versions can support demand analytics, prioritisation and resource insights.

INNOVATION 06

Modular Architecture

Emergency, resources, donors, camps and assistant modules can evolve independently.

Safety & Responsibility: RaktSathi is a coordination and resource-discovery prototype. It does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood. It does not guarantee blood availability and does not make medical compatibility, donor eligibility or treatment decisions. Actual blood collection, testing, storage, compatibility assessment and transfusion are handled by authorised healthcare professionals and facilities.
