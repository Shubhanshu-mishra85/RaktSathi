04

Verify

Actual donation and blood issue procedures remain with authorized healthcare facilities.

🛡️ VitalLoop Safety & Responsibility
VitalLoop is a healthcare technology prototype focused on discovery, information and coordination. It does not store, test, transport or release blood. Actual donor screening, blood testing, compatibility verification, storage, donation and issue/release are handled by authorized blood centres and healthcare professionals.
