RESPONSIBLE TECHNOLOGY

Designed for Safer Coordination

RaktSathi is designed as a coordination and discovery layer. It does not replace authorised medical or blood-centre processes.

🔐

Privacy-Aware

Sensitive information should be collected only when necessary and handled responsibly.

🏥

Facility Verification

Blood availability and final clinical decisions must be confirmed by authorised facilities.

⚠️

No False Guarantees

Potential resource information is presented as discoverable information, not guaranteed availability.

🧑‍⚕️

Human Oversight

Actual collection, testing, storage, compatibility, release and transfusion remain under authorised healthcare processes.
