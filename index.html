BLOOD RESOURCE NETWORK

Connecting People With Relevant Resources

VitalLoop is designed as a discovery and coordination layer to make healthcare navigation easier when people need relevant blood resources.

✓ Search by blood group and location
✓ Raise structured blood requests
✓ Coordinate potential donors
✓ Verify availability with authorized centres
🩸

Every Drop Matters

VitalLoop does not itself collect, test, store, transport, release or distribute blood. Actual donation, testing, compatibility and transfusion remain under authorized healthcare facilities and trained professionals.
