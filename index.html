A simple technology-assisted journey for discovering and coordinating healthcare resources.

1

Raise a Need

Enter blood group, component and relevant request information.

2

Discover

Find relevant authorized blood centres and potential donor resources.

3

4

Receive Care

Actual donation, testing, compatibility and blood release remain with authorized facilities.

🛡️

Safety & Responsible Use

RaktSathi is a discovery and coordination technology prototype. It does not store, test, transport, release or distribute blood. Actual blood collection, testing, compatibility assessment, storage and release are performed by authorized blood centres and healthcare facilities according to applicable medical protocols. Blood availability shown by a prototype should never be treated as a guarantee.
