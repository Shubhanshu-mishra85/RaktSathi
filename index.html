Connecting People With Relevant Resources

VitalLoop is designed as a discovery and coordination layer. It does not itself store, test, transport or release blood.

✓ Search by blood group and location
✓ Raise structured blood requests
✓ Explore potential donor coordination
✓ Verify availability with authorized centres

Every Drop Matters

Actual donation, testing, storage, compatibility and transfusion remain under authorized healthcare facilities and trained professionals.
