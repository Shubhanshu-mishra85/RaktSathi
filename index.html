The Problem

Finding the right blood resource can become a race against time.

Families may need to contact multiple places, search different sources and coordinate donors while dealing with an already stressful situation.

Traditional journey

Information can be fragmented across hospitals, blood centres, phone calls and personal contacts.

Safety & Trust

Technology should support healthcare, not replace it.

Important prototype boundaries

VitalLoop is designed as a discovery, matching and coordination layer. It does not independently perform medical decisions.

✓ VitalLoop does not collect or test blood.
✓ VitalLoop does not store, transport or issue blood.
✓ Blood compatibility decisions remain with qualified professionals.
✓ Demo resource availability is not guaranteed or real-time.
✓ The AI assistant provides educational information, not diagnosis.
✓ Urgent medical situations require professional emergency care.
