THE CHALLENGE

Blood Emergencies Need Coordination, Not Just Search

During an emergency, finding a possible blood resource is only one part of the problem. Patients and families may also need donor discovery, facility coordination, verification and status visibility.

🔎

Fragmented Discovery

Information may exist across different facilities, contacts and platforms.

⏱️

Time Pressure

Emergency situations require a clear and structured path to the next step.

⚠️

Verification Gap

Online availability information can change and should be confirmed directly with the relevant authorized facility.
