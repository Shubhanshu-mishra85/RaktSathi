⚠️ Important Safety Information

VitalLoop is a healthcare technology prototype and discovery/coordination layer. It does not itself collect, test, store, transport, release or distribute blood. Actual donation, testing, compatibility, storage and transfusion are handled by authorized healthcare facilities and trained professionals. Search results shown by this prototype do not represent guaranteed real-time blood availability.
