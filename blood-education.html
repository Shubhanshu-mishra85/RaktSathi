FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Blood Awareness FAQs

What are blood groups?

Blood groups are classifications of blood based on specific markers on blood cells. The ABO and Rh systems are commonly used.

Why is blood verification important?

Online data can change. Before relying on availability information, contact the relevant authorized blood centre or healthcare facility.

Can RaktSathi decide compatibility?

No. RaktSathi is a coordination and discovery prototype. Final compatibility and clinical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

Does RaktSathi store blood?

No. RaktSathi does not collect, test, store, transport or release blood. Those activities remain with authorized healthcare facilities.
