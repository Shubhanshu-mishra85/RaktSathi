Blood Components

Blood can be separated into components for different clinical requirements.

Red Blood Cells

Carry oxygen throughout the body and may be used in situations involving significant blood loss or anaemia, according to clinical assessment.

Platelets

Help with blood clotting and may be required for patients with certain platelet-related conditions.

Plasma

The liquid portion of blood containing proteins and other substances important to circulation and clotting.

Cryoprecipitate

A plasma-derived component containing selected clotting proteins and used for specific clinical indications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is blood-group verification important?

Blood-group information is an important part of transfusion safety. Final blood-group testing, compatibility assessment and transfusion decisions are performed by authorised healthcare professionals.

Does finding a donor guarantee blood availability?

No. A registered donor is only a potential resource. Availability, eligibility, screening and donation must be confirmed through the appropriate authorised facility and professionals.

Does RaktSathi provide or sell blood?

No. RaktSathi is a discovery and coordination prototype. It does not collect, test, store, transport, release or transfuse blood.

Can online information replace medical advice?

No. Educational information is not a substitute for assessment or advice from qualified healthcare professionals.

What should I do if an emergency request is created?

Coordinate with the treating hospital or authorised blood centre. RaktSathi can help structure discovery and coordination, but it cannot guarantee a medical resource or emergency outcome.
