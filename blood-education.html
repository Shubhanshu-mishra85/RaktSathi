Blood Education

Understand blood groups, blood components, donation basics and important safety information.

Why Blood Awareness Matters

Understanding blood groups and donation basics can help people make informed decisions and coordinate with authorised blood centres and healthcare facilities when blood support is needed.

🩸 Blood Groups

The commonly recognised ABO blood groups are A, B, AB and O. Each can also be positive or negative depending on the Rh factor.

🧬 Blood Components

Blood can be separated into components, each serving different clinical purposes.

Red Blood Cells Carry oxygen throughout the body.
Platelets Help with blood clotting.
Plasma The liquid part of blood that carries cells and many dissolved substances.
Cryoprecipitate A plasma-derived component containing specific clotting proteins.

❤️ Blood Donation Basics

Blood donation is a voluntary process carried out through authorised blood centres and healthcare facilities.

Before Donation

  • Follow the instructions provided by the authorised centre.
  • Provide accurate health and medical information to staff.
  • Follow any food, hydration or rest guidance given by the centre.

After Donation

  • Follow the post-donation instructions provided by staff.
  • Rest if advised.
  • Contact healthcare professionals if you have concerns.

✅ Donor Eligibility

Donation eligibility depends on factors such as age, health, haemoglobin level, medications, recent illness and other requirements set by the authorised blood centre.

Always allow qualified healthcare staff to determine whether you are eligible to donate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a blood group?

A blood group identifies characteristics of blood cells. The ABO system and Rh factor are commonly used to classify blood.

Can anyone donate blood?

No. Eligibility is determined by authorised blood-centre staff according to applicable medical requirements.

Does RaktSathi collect blood?

No. RaktSathi is a discovery and coordination platform. Blood collection, testing, storage and transfusion are handled by authorised blood centres and healthcare professionals.

Does registering as a donor guarantee a donation?

No. Registration only indicates potential donor interest. Actual availability and eligibility must be confirmed through the appropriate healthcare facility.

Where should I verify medical information?

For personal medical questions or donation eligibility, consult qualified healthcare professionals or an authorised blood centre.

Important Safety Notice:
This page provides general educational information and is not medical advice. Blood donation eligibility, compatibility, testing, collection, storage and transfusion must be determined by qualified healthcare professionals and authorised facilities.
