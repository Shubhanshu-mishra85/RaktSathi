RESPONSIBLE TECHNOLOGY

What RaktSathi Does — and Does Not Do

✓

RaktSathi Can Help With

  • ✓ Healthcare resource discovery
  • ✓ Blood resource search
  • ✓ Emergency request coordination
  • ✓ Request status tracking
  • ✓ General health education
  • ✓ Navigation toward professional support
🛡️

RaktSathi Does Not

  • ✕ Diagnose medical conditions
  • ✕ Prescribe medicines
  • ✕ Replace doctors or pharmacists
  • ✕ Decide blood compatibility
  • ✕ Collect or store blood
  • ✕ Guarantee blood availability
