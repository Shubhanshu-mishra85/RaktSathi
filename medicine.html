HEALTHCARE SUPPORT & CARE GUIDE

Medicine & Care Information

General healthcare guidance to help users understand the right coordination pathway during a health-related need.

Important: RaktSathi does not prescribe medicines, diagnose diseases or replace a doctor. Medicine selection, dosage, interactions and treatment decisions must be made by a qualified healthcare professional.

Healthcare Support Areas

Use the appropriate healthcare pathway for your need.

🩺

Medical Consultation

💊

Prescription medicines should be taken only according to professional medical advice and the prescribed instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can RaktSathi recommend a medicine?

No. RaktSathi is not a prescribing system. Medicine selection and treatment decisions require qualified professional assessment.

Can I change a prescribed dose using this website?

No. Do not change, stop or start a prescription based only on information from this website. Consult the prescribing healthcare professional.

What if my need is a blood emergency?

Use the Emergency Request module to structure the blood requirement and coordinate with the treating healthcare facility or authorised blood centre.

Does RaktSathi replace a hospital?

No. RaktSathi is a coordination and discovery prototype. It does not replace hospitals, blood centres or healthcare professionals.
